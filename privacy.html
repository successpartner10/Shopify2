Privacy

Your Shopify screen can show names, payouts, and passwords. We treat it like someone standing next to you.

  • Screenshots and tab captures are read in this browser. They are not uploaded to a Storescope server.
  • OCR is vendored Tesseract (English) and can run offline after the first cache.
  • History stores written steps only — never the image.
  • Optional Gemini / Grok is off unless you turn it on. Then only scrubbed banner text is sent, from your browser, with a key that stays in localStorage.
  • We do not request Shopify Admin API scopes. We do not log into your store.
